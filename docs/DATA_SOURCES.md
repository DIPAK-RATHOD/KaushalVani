# Government Data Sources & Provenance Policy

KaushalVani enforces strict data integrity: LLMs are never permitted to generate qualification titles, eligibility rules, or job openings.

| Data Source | Entity Type | Official URL | Authority / Publisher |
|---|---|---|---|
| National Qualification Register (NQR) | Qualifications & NSQF Levels | https://nqr.gov.in | National Skill Development Agency (NSDA) |
| Skill India Digital (SID) | Accredited ITIs & Training Centres | https://skillindia.gov.in | Ministry of Skill Development (MSDE) |
| National Career Service (NCS) | Verified Wage Job Openings | https://ncs.gov.in | Ministry of Labour & Employment |
| BHASHINI AI Gateway | Speech STT / TTS Engine | https://bhashini.gov.in | MeitY |
| PM-AJAY GIA Guidelines | Grant-in-Aid Livelihood Framework | https://pmajay.gov.in | Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment |
| Open Government Data (OGD) | District Workforce Statistics | https://data.gov.in | National Informatics Centre (NIC) |
